# Win/loss analysis report

**Analysis date:** April 2026
**Transcripts analyzed:** 8
**Outcome breakdown:** 5 wins / 3 losses

---

## Executive summary

Across 8 analyzed calls (5 wins, 3 losses), product-market fit is strong for mid-market teams seeking self-serve analytics. Wins consistently cite "ease of setup" and "no SQL required" (4 of 5 wins). Losses cluster around enterprise requirements — missing SSO and audit logs mentioned in all 3 losses. Pricing was not a factor in any loss.

**Strategic recommendations:**
1. Prioritize enterprise security features (SSO, audit logs) to unlock enterprise segment
2. Double down on "no SQL required" messaging — strongest resonance across wins
3. Train sales on handling "security review" objection with roadmap response

## Insights by dimension

### 1. Product

**Pattern:** Ease of setup and self-serve capability drive wins; missing enterprise security features drive losses.

**Evidence (wins):**
> "We were up and running in 2 hours. No SQL, no engineering dependency. That's exactly what we needed."
> — Champion (Head of RevOps), Acme Corp, Win

> "The dashboard builder is intuitive. My team was building reports on day one without training."
> — Champion (VP Marketing), TechStartup Inc, Win

**Evidence (losses):**
> "Your product is great, but without SSO and audit logs, we can't get through security review."
> — Economic Buyer (VP Engineering), Enterprise Co, Loss

**Frequency:** Setup ease: 4 of 5 wins | Security gaps: 3 of 3 losses
**Confidence:** High

**Opportunity:** Add SSO and audit logs to product roadmap. Create "enterprise security" FAQ for sales to handle objection with timeline commitment.

---

### 2. Messaging

**Pattern:** Wins cite clear differentiation from competitors; losses hear generic positioning.

**Evidence (wins):**
> "When you said 'analytics without the SQL tax,' I knew this was for my team, not my engineers."
> — Champion (Head of Growth), ScaleUp Co, Win

**Evidence (losses):**
> "Honestly, your website sounded like every other analytics tool. I couldn't tell why you were different until the demo."
> — Champion (Director of Analytics), MidMarket Ltd, Loss

**Frequency:** 4 of 8 calls | **Confidence:** Medium

**Opportunity:** Lead with "no SQL required" differentiation on homepage and in sales outreach. Current messaging buries this below feature lists.

---

### 3. GTM/Sales

**Pattern:** Tailored demos that address specific use cases win; generic walkthroughs lose.

**Evidence (wins):**
> "You actually showed us our use case — marketing attribution without engineering tickets. That sold me."
> — Champion (CMO), GrowthCo, Win

**Evidence (losses):**
> "The demo covered features we didn't need and skipped the one thing we asked about — cohort analysis."
> — Economic Buyer (Head of Product), DataDriven Inc, Loss

**Frequency:** 5 of 8 calls | **Confidence:** High

**Opportunity:** Implement pre-demo discovery checklist. Map top 3 use cases per persona and build demo tracks.

---

### 4. Pricing

**Pattern:** Pricing is not a friction point. Wins see it as fair value.

**Evidence:**
> "At $14 per seat, it's a no-brainer compared to Amplitude's enterprise pricing."
> — Champion (VP Marketing), ScaleUp Co, Win

**Frequency:** 3 of 8 calls (pricing only discussed when prompted) | **Confidence:** Medium

**Opportunity:** No immediate pricing changes needed. Consider adding "vs Amplitude pricing" comparison on pricing page.

---

### 5. Competition

**Pattern:** DataPulse wins on speed and simplicity vs. Amplitude/Mixpanel; loses on enterprise compliance.

**Evidence (wins):**
> "We evaluated Amplitude but it took weeks to set up. You took 2 hours."
> — Champion (Head of RevOps), Acme Corp, Win

**Evidence (losses):**
> "Mixpanel already has SSO and SOC2. We can't justify a tool that doesn't pass our security checklist."
> — Economic Buyer (VP Engineering), Enterprise Co, Loss

**Frequency:** 6 of 8 calls mention competitors | **Confidence:** High

**Opportunity:** Build competitive battlecard: "DataPulse vs Amplitude" focused on time-to-value. Address Mixpanel SSO gap in product roadmap.

---

### 6. Customer context

**Pattern:** Wins have urgent, time-bound analytics needs; losses are evaluating without pressure.

**Evidence (wins):**
> "We just hired a growth team and they need dashboards by next month. Can't wait 3 months for Amplitude setup."
> — Champion (VP Marketing), TechStartup Inc, Win

**Evidence (losses):**
> "We're evaluating tools for next quarter. No rush — we'll make a decision in 6-8 weeks."
> — Champion (Director of Analytics), MidMarket Ltd, Loss

**Frequency:** 5 of 8 calls | **Confidence:** High

**Opportunity:** Target companies with active hiring signals (growth team expansion) in outbound. Deprioritize accounts with long evaluation timelines.
